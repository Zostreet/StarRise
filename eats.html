Our Delivery Roadmap

  1. Vendor Network: Restaurant profiles, food trucks, menus, and business verification.
  2. Pickup Ordering: Customer carts, secure checkout, order confirmations, and merchant order management.
  3. Delivery Integration: Evaluate approved third-party courier services where available.
  4. StarRise Drivers: Develop driver applications, screening, insurance, dispatch, and payouts when operationally ready.
  5. Events: Food vendors, concessions, sponsors, and special offers for StarRise events.

No affiliation with DoorDash, Uber, or other delivery providers is implied unless a formal agreement is established.
