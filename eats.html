Our Development Roadmap

  1. Food Marketplace: Let visitors discover participating food businesses and browse available menus.
  2. Vendor Management: Allow registered vendors to manage menu items, pricing, and availability.
  3. Shopping Cart: Develop item selection, quantities, and order totals.
  4. Pickup Ordering: Add secure checkout and order management after testing and payment approval.
  5. Delivery Integration: Explore approved delivery-service partnerships and integrations.
  6. StarRise Delivery: Establish driver screening, insurance, dispatch, compliance, and payouts before launching delivery operations.
  7. Events: Connect food vendors and sponsors with StarRise entertainment events.

StarRise has not announced a partnership with DoorDash, Uber, or another delivery company.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I browse food menus?

Yes. Visit the Food Marketplace to explore menus that participating vendors have published.

Can customers order food now?

Not yet. Ordering and checkout will be introduced after vendor onboarding, payment approval, and system testing.

Can my restaurant join now?

Yes. Create a StarRise vendor account. Eligible registered vendors can access the menu dashboard. Registration does not guarantee approval for future ordering services.

Can I apply to become a driver?

Yes. Submit a driver interest application using your StarRise account. Applications do not guarantee employment or delivery assignments.

Can sponsors join?

Yes. Create a StarRise partner account to explore sponsorship opportunities. Formal agreements are handled separately.
