Our Development Roadmap

  1. Recruitment: Build our vendor, sponsor, and prospective driver network.
  2. Vendor Profiles: Add verified food businesses and menu management.
  3. Pickup Ordering: Introduce carts, payment processing, and order tracking after testing and approval.
  4. Delivery Integration: Explore approved delivery-service integrations.
  5. StarRise Delivery Operations: Establish screening, insurance, dispatch, compliance, and payouts before activating any independent delivery program.
  6. Events: Connect food vendors and sponsors with StarRise entertainment events.

StarRise has not announced a partnership with DoorDash, Uber, or any other delivery company.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can customers order food now?

Not yet. Customer ordering will be introduced after the necessary technology, vendor onboarding, and payment testing are completed.

Can my restaurant join now?

Yes. You can create a vendor account today. Registration does not guarantee approval or immediate access to ordering features.

Can I apply to become a driver?

Yes. Visit the driver interest application and sign in to your StarRise account. Applications do not guarantee employment or delivery assignments.

Can sponsors join?

Yes. Create a StarRise partner account to begin exploring sponsorship opportunities. Any formal agreement will be handled separately.
