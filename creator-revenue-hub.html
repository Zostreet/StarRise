8. Fam Entertainment Creator Rights Principles

  • Creators retain ownership of their original work unless a separate, clearly disclosed agreement states otherwise.
  • Fam Entertainment does not receive ownership simply because a creator uses StarRise.
  • Collaborators should document songwriting, production, recording, artwork, and other contributions.
  • Any licensing, distribution, promotion, or revenue-sharing arrangement must have clear terms and creator authorization.
  • Creators must have a way to review their agreements and ownership records.
  • No guaranteed streams, followers, placements, sales, or earnings.
