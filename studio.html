Ready to record.

The count-in is excluded from your take. Existing tracks play from Record From, and the new take starts there. The click is not added to exports; use headphones to keep it out of the microphone. Adjust Start in Song after recording if your device adds latency.

Session Tracks

Start in Song positions a track. Trim Start and Trim End select the audio to play. Original audio stays available with Restore Full Audio. Undo keeps the last 20 track edits while this session is open.

Multitrack Mixer & Effects

Each track has independent volume, pan, low-cut, EQ, presence, sibilance softening, compression, reverb and delay. Vocal tracks also have tone presets. Mix changes apply on the next playback and to new exports.

Export Your Music

Exports are uncompressed 16-bit WAV. Your audio is processed locally in this browser.

Project Backup

Full project backups include recordings, imported tracks, sample-pad audio, and mix settings. Share the backup file to let another creator open and continue your project.

Session Information

Choose Save Project to keep this session, including its audio, on this device. Choose Load Project to reopen that saved session. Browser storage can be cleared or become full; download a full project backup to keep a separate copy or move to another device.
