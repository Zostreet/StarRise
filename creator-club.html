Safety comes first

A creator space with clear boundaries.

This is planned as a recreation and creation club, not a communal residence. The owner lives at the property. Overnight stays would require individual approval; all other visitors would follow approved access, booking, and guest rules. Private residential areas would remain off-limits.

Before opening, StarRise must confirm applicable zoning, occupancy, insurance, fire safety, business-use, and privacy requirements. Filming without consent, harassment, and unapproved guests would not be permitted.
