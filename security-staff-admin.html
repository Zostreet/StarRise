Youth Safeguarding Rules

Protecting young creators is a priority.

Personnel working with minors must complete appropriate screening and safeguarding training before being assigned youth-facing responsibilities.

  • No unauthorized private one-on-one communication between staff and minors.
  • Use approved, monitored communication channels with parent or guardian involvement where appropriate.
  • Apply appropriate adult supervision and two-adult practices during youth activities.
  • Document safety concerns promptly and objectively.
  • Report suspected abuse or exploitation to the appropriate authorities as required by law.
  • Call 911 for immediate danger.
  • Do not upload explicit material or unnecessary sensitive information into internal reports.

These rules are an operational starting point. They do not replace formal safeguarding training, applicable reporting laws, or professional advice.
