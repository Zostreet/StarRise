Access protection rules

Approval in this registry is an administrative personnel status only. It does not automatically authorize viewing, modifying, or investigating security incidents.

Personnel must complete general security and youth safeguarding training before approval. Screening, legal requirements, and role-specific authorization must be handled separately.

Emergency threats require appropriate emergency services. Suspected child abuse or exploitation must be reported as required by applicable law.
