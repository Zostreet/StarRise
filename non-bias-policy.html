Our Promise

Non-Bias Policy

Fam Entertainment LLC is a non-biased company. We don't choose talent by background, race, gender, age, or who you know — we choose by the work.

Effective November 21, 2026

Everybody Shines

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not. We exist to change that.

StarRise was built so that independent musicians, performers, creators, vendors, and businesses get a real shot — no matter where they come from, what they look like, or who they know. Everybody eats. Every talent deserves a chance. If you put in the work, you deserve a stage, an audience, and a real opportunity.

What Non-Biased Means Here

No decision at Fam Entertainment LLC is made on the basis of race, ethnicity, national origin, gender, sexual orientation, age, disability, religion, background, or personal connections. We do not favor one group over another, and we do not exclude anyone from an opportunity because of who they are.

Where It Applies

This policy covers everything we do:

  • Artist features, showcases, bookings, and lineups
  • Studio services, promotion, and creator support
  • Hiring, staffing, and team roles
  • Partnerships, vendors, and business directory listings
  • Community moderation and member access
  • Events, workshops, and youth-facing programming

How We Choose

We choose by the work: the quality of the craft, the effort behind it, readiness for the opportunity, and fit for what the moment calls for. That is the whole standard. We will not ask you to be anyone other than who you are to earn a place here.

What We Won't Do

  • No favoritism for friends, family, or insiders.
  • No pay-to-play — buying your way ahead of someone else's work.
  • No gatekeeping by genre, style, or background.
  • No opportunity reserved for one group while others are shut out.

If Something Feels Wrong

If you believe you were treated unfairly in any StarRise or Fam Entertainment LLC decision, tell us. Reach out through our Contact & Support page, and we will review what happened and respond. We take that seriously — this policy only means something if we stand behind it.

Questions about this policy?
