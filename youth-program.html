StarRise Youth Arts

A creative outlet.
A place to begin.

Young artists can explore singing, rap, songwriting, beatmaking, art, acting and storytelling. Parents and teachers can practice alongside them and help their ideas grow.

What is available now

  • A separate creative space: original activities and tools for writing, beats, drawing, story planning and stage practice.
  • No child account needed: the youth page does not sign children into StarRise, create public profiles, take payments, offer private messages or accept uploads.
  • Creations remain in the current tab: users can export text, PNG artwork and WAV beats to their own device. Save before leaving; there is no cloud save or recovery.
  • No adult feed in the youth space: it does not load member posts, marketplace listings, messages or third-party video embeds. The main adult platform retains its existing features.

Child profiles, shared uploads and a public youth showcase are not open. Any future service that receives children's information needs an appropriate guardian verification and permission process, private storage, review, removal controls and supervision before it is offered.

Opening this page or creating an adult account does not enroll a child or record verified guardian consent.

For parents and guardians

Explore activities together. Talk about the choices behind a song, drawing or story instead of judging it by popularity. A young person can contribute by writing, drawing or production; solo performance is optional.

  • Help creators use imaginary details instead of their real names, school, address or contact information.
  • Save copies to a device your family controls. Review a file before sharing it anywhere else.
  • The youth section has its own navigation. The main StarRise service remains for adults 18+; use your family's device and browsing settings when appropriate.
  • Young creators retain their original work. Using these tools does not upload the work, transfer ownership or create a commercial agreement.

A proposal for discussion

A supervised school arts pilot

We are inviting a school partner to help shape a first StarRise Youth Arts pilot. A host school, dates, facilitators and funding will be agreed before student registration opens. This page does not announce a scheduled or funded program.

Suggested starting format
Participants15–20 middle or high school students; year groups agreed with the school
ScheduleSix weekly sessions of 90 minutes
SettingA school-approved room with school involvement in supervision
Final pieceAn original group performance or recording, shared privately or at a school-approved event

Suggested six-week creative journey

  1. Find your voice: try acting, vocal and rhythm exercises; choose a creative or production role.
  2. Build a story: write a scene, verse, lyric or spoken-word piece.
  3. Practice performance: explore delivery, rhythm and expression with constructive feedback.
  4. Create together: develop an original group piece and try simple recording or beatmaking.
  5. Rehearse and refine: assign production roles and make final edits.
  6. Share and reflect: use a school-approved setting and discuss what students want to try next.

Before sessions begin, StarRise and the school would agree delivery, staffing and screening, supervision, accessibility, emergency procedures, permissions, costs and handling of concerns. Student registration and guardian permissions would go through the school's agreed process. Public media permissions and any commercial release require separate arrangements.
