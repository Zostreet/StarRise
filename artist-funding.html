How StarRise artist advances work

An advance is money up front against the streaming income your music is already earning. You spend it on your career — studio time, videos, marketing, touring — and it is repaid from a share of your future streaming revenue only.

1

Check your estimate

Enter your trailing 12-month streaming income and pick a term. The estimator shows a range, not a promise.

2

Apply in minutes

Share your streaming profiles, your numbers, and a distribution report if you have one. No fee to apply.

3

We review it

The StarRise team reviews your application and your streaming history. We will contact you at the email you provide.

4

Terms are set with a funding partner

If your application moves forward, final terms are set with a funding partner. You keep 100% of your masters and ownership — repayment comes only from a share of streaming revenue.

You choose how much of your monthly streaming income you keep during the term (50% to 90%). The rest goes toward repaying the advance. Income from merch, live shows, physical sales, sync, and publishing is not part of repayment.
