Your creativity. Your privacy.

How this space works.

Effective October 11, 2026. This notice covers the free Young Creators learning page and its creative tools.

You don't need an account

The youth page does not ask for your name, email, age, school, address, photo or account details. It doesn't sign you into the main StarRise community or use an adult account that may already be signed in on the device.

Keep real names, school information and contact details out of your work. Use imaginary details in songs, scenes and stories.

Your creations stay with you

Writing, drawings, beat patterns, story plans and activity progress stay in the current browser tab. The tools don't send them to StarRise, Supabase, PayPal, an AI service or another person. There is no cloud save, sharing feed or recovery feature.

Closing or reloading the page clears the work and progress in that tab. Save a copy first if you want to keep it. Saved text, PNG and WAV files go to your device through its browser; your device or family backup settings may handle those files. Ask a parent or teacher before sharing a saved copy anywhere else.

Creating with these tools does not transfer ownership of your original work to StarRise.

No ads, private messages or uploads here

This youth area does not add advertising trackers, analytics, persistent user identifiers, cookies or local storage. It does not load adult member content, third-party videos, public profiles, comments, payments or private messages. It does not request camera, microphone or location access.

The website host receives ordinary web requests, such as your network address and browser request information, to deliver and secure the page. This notice does not mean that hosting produces no technical records. The Young Creators tools themselves send no creations or activity reports over the network.

Grown-up information and help

Parents and teachers can open the adult information page to learn about the proposed school arts program and contact StarRise. That page links to the main website, which uses its own account and privacy practices. Child accounts and shared uploads are not available.

If a parent believes a child has submitted personal information through another StarRise feature, the parent can use the adult support route to ask for review or deletion. Do not send additional child information in the initial enquiry.

Parent & teacher help

Future changes

If the youth service changes to receive children's information, accounts or shared work, it will need a separate privacy notice and an appropriate guardian permission process before those features open. This notice will be updated when the available features change.
