Concord, North Carolina

Art Walk on Union

Saturday, April 10, 2027 · 11 AM–4 PM

Downtown Concord will celebrate handmade art and regional makers, with live music, food trucks, downtown shops and restaurants, and an Open Studios Day at ClearWater Arts Center & Studios.

StarRise is sharing this community event to help local artists, makers, and art supporters discover ways to participate and show up for the creative community.

Artists and makers: Spring 2027 vendor applications are listed as open through November 20, 2026. The organizer is seeking regional artists and makers who create handcrafted artwork. Review the official requirements and apply directly through the Cabarrus Arts Council.

Event Details & Artist Applications

Community spotlight only. Art Walk on Union is presented by the City of Concord and Cabarrus Arts Council. StarRise is not an event organizer or official event partner.
