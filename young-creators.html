Your scene

You are the first explorer to find a garden on the moon. Show the moment you discover it.

Try it once with excitement, once with surprise, and once with quiet confidence.

  1. Relax your shoulders and take one comfortable breath.
  2. Choose a clear beginning, middle, and ending.
  3. Try the scene for 30 seconds. You can also draw or write it.
  4. Notice one thing you liked and one thing you want to try next.
