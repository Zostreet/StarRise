5. Ownership Rules

  • Restrict access to confidential inventions and trade secrets.
  • Have qualified counsel review important ownership and filing decisions.

This registry is an administrative record. It does not file applications, create legal ownership, or replace legal advice. Avoid entering passwords, private keys, sensitive invention details or confidential trade secrets into an unprotected browser record.
